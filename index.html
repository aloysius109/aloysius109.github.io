About

Evidence that helps people make better decisions.

I’m a data and analytics leader currently heading Business Intelligence at Salutem Care and Education, where I’m building an Azure-hosted Databricks data platform for trusted reporting, governance and self-service analysis.

Earlier roles across the Home Office, NHS Digital, HMRC and the Ministry of Justice combined analytical leadership with hands-on work in evaluation, predictive modelling and public-sector statistics. I hold a PhD in Environmental Science and enjoy making complex evidence clear and useful.
